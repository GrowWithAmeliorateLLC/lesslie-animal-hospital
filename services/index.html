What we do

Everything your pet needs, under one roof.

Full-service veterinary medicine for dogs, cats and exotics — plus the boarding and grooming most clinics send you elsewhere for. All of it right here on Riverview Road.

Wellness & Vaccinations

Annual exams and core and lifestyle vaccines for cats and dogs, plus preventive care that keeps pets thriving at every age.

Dentistry

Professional cleanings under anesthesia, extractions, and home-care guidance for healthier teeth and gums.

Surgery

Spays, neuters and routine surgical procedures — with ultrasound and a full workup on site, and a technician monitoring your pet the entire time.

Therapy Laser

Our newest service. Drug-free laser therapy to ease pain, calm inflammation and speed healing.

Sick & Urgent Visits

Same-practice care when your pet isn't feeling right — seen by people who already know their history.

Grooming

Baths, brush-outs, nail trims and full grooms from a groomer with more than 30 years of experience.

Boarding

Comfortable, supervised stays for dogs and cats, with cuddle time, walks and playtime add-ons.

Hospice & End-of-Life Care

Deciding on hospice care or euthanasia is a tender, private decision. Our staff will walk you through it with reverence and patience, and help you keep your pet comfortable through their final moments.

Through our partner Rest Assured Animal Cremations, cremation and memorial options are available — with or without return of ashes, or you're welcome to take your pet home.
