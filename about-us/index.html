About us

A family caring for your family's pets.

Serving York County since 1997, Lesslie Animal Hospital has been a second home to thousands of pets — for wellness care, surgery, boarding and grooming alike. We're proudly family-owned, and we treat every patient like our own.

What we stand for

Caring people, caring for pets

Our mission

To provide the very best veterinary pet care possible, while extending a comforting hand in a calm and compassionate atmosphere.

Our vision

To be the go-to animal hospital in the Rock Hill area — promoting animal health through wellness care, vaccinations and world-class service.

Our purpose

To give animals the resources they need to live healthy lives, and to answer every question their families have along the way.

Hablamos español. We have Spanish-speaking staff in our office so we can better serve the clients who prefer it. Language is never an obstacle for our patients. Tenemos empleados que hablan español en nuestra oficina para poder servir mejor a los clientes que lo prefieran. ¡Que el idioma no sea un obstáculo para nuestros pacientes!
