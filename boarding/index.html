Before you come

Vaccines required for boarding

Please bring proof of vaccines at drop-off.

Cats

  • FVRC-P vaccine
  • Bordetella vaccine
  • Rabies vaccine
  • Profender (dewormer)

Dogs

  • DHLPP vaccine
  • Bordetella vaccine
  • Rabies vaccine
  • Intestinal worm exam
Overdue on vaccines? If a boarder is overdue, or no vaccine history is provided at drop-off, we'll update the needed vaccines during their stay. Please note that Lesslie Animal Hospital is not responsible or liable for any illness a pet may contract during boarding if they are not up to date. We strongly recommend updating vaccines 3 to 4 weeks before boarding so they have time to take effect.
