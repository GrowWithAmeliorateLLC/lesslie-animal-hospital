Exotic pet & zoo medicine

When most vets say "we don't see those," we say bring them in. Dr. Setzer cares for small exotics in the clinic — and serves as the veterinarian for Tiger World, treating tigers, lions, bears, and primates. Few practices in the Carolinas offer this depth of exotic experience.

Exotics we treat

Rabbits · guinea pigs · ferrets · rodents · and other small mammal exotics. Some clients drive an hour or more for primate care. (We do not see reptiles, birds, or large animals.)

The Tiger World connection

Dr. Setzer is the chief staff veterinarian for Tiger World, an animal-conservation and education center in Rockwell, NC. That same expertise is available to your exotic pets.
