Exotics & zoo medicine

When other vets say "we don't see those."

We do. Dr. Setzer cares for small exotics right here in the clinic — and serves as the staff veterinarian for Tiger World, treating tigers, lions, bears and primates. Few practices in the Carolinas offer this depth of exotic experience.

Exotics we treat

  • Rabbits
  • Guinea pigs
  • Ferrets
  • Rodents
  • Other small mammal exotics

Some clients drive an hour or more to see us. If you're not sure whether we treat your pet, just call and ask.

What we don't see

So you don't make a wasted trip: we do not treat reptiles, birds, or large animals.

If your pet falls into one of those groups, call us anyway — we're glad to point you toward someone who can help.

Beyond the ordinary

The vet trusted by Tiger World

Tiger World is an animal conservation and education center in Rockwell, NC. Dr. Setzer is their chief staff veterinarian — and that same expertise comes to your exotic pet, right here in Rock Hill.

Caring for big cats, bears and primates takes a different kind of training and a steady hand. It's the same care your rabbit or ferret gets when you bring them through our door.
